A program that writes one original one-star review (171 characters, under the 280-character cap) in the voice of a houseplant reviewing its human owner, to `/output/entry.txt`, for make.fun's "One Star, Written in Sap" bounty. The plant files a real grievance — watered daily out of guilt, never out of love, then photographed for social media captioned "thriving" — and earns its single star with the sting in the last four words: *"One star. My human waters me every day out of guilt, never out of love, then photographs me captioned 'thriving.' I am not thriving. I am drowning with excellent lighting."* It runs offline, reads nothing from the network, and writes the same text on every run.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
